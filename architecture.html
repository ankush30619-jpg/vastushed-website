The 6-Phase Design-to-Build Lifecycle

How VASTUSHED synthesizes ancient spatial harmony with modern parametric BIM engineering.

PHASE 01 // SURVEY CLIMATOLOGY

Solar & Geotechnical Survey

Digital total-station site topography, soil load-bearing capacity tests, annual sun-path trajectory analysis, and seasonal wind azimuth mapping.

Deliverable: Site Feasibility Dossier
PHASE 02 // VASTU GRID MANDALA ALIGN

Vedic Spatial Zoning & Layout

Overlaying the 9-Sector Vastu Purusha Mandala onto cardinal axes. Room allocations mapped to elemental forces (Water in NE, Fire in SE, Earth in SW).

Deliverable: 2D Schematic Floor Plans
PHASE 03 // VISUALIZATION 4K RAY-TRACING

Photorealistic 3D Renders

High-definition 3D exterior elevations, dusk architectural lighting simulations, and interior walk-throughs showcasing precise natural material textures.

Deliverable: 4K CGI Renders & Walkthrough
PHASE 04 // ENGINEERING GFC DRAWINGS

Structural & MEP Working Sets

Seismic rebar schedules, column-beam sections, concealed plumbing raceways, electrical load balancing, and central HVAC ducting schematics.

Deliverable: GFC Construction Blueprint Set
PHASE 05 // QUANTITIES AUDITED BOQ

Material Specs & Audited BOQ

Itemized Bill of Quantities down to kilograms of steel, cubic meters of concrete, square feet of Italian marble, joinery hardware, and transparent cost estimates.

Deliverable: Transparent Procurement BOQ
PHASE 06 // EXECUTION ON-SITE QA

Site Calibration & Supervision

Periodic site inspections by senior architects, laser-level alignment checks, snag clearing, and final Vastu energy harmonization before client move-in.

Deliverable: Zero-Snag Handover Certification
