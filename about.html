The VASTUSHED Foundation

Why We Dismantled Industry Silos

In traditional building practice, an architect produces aesthetic drawings without on-site builder feedback. The contractor arrives later and alters details due to construction hurdles or cost issues. Finally, a Vastu consultant is invited when the house is half-built, often prescribing fear-based remedies or costly demolitions.

At VASTUSHED, architecture, interiors, construction, and Vastu are not isolated services—they are integrated parts of one synchronized, transparent process.

Design Spaces That Are Practical to Build

Our architects design with direct contractor knowledge of structural spans, local materials, concrete grades, and MEP ducting. What is drawn on paper is fully executable on-site.

Execute Projects That Respect Original Design Intent

Because our own in-house construction team builds the structure, architectural proportions, material textures, and luxury joinery are preserved down to the millimeter without dilution.

Apply Vastu That Complements Modern Lifestyles

Our approach is professional, transparent, and grounded in experience—not theoretical or dogmatic. We harmonize solar physics, wind ventilation, and geomagnetic wellness without superstition.
