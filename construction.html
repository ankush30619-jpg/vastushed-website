Turnkey Execution

VASTUSHED operates as a direct contractor and builder, offering complete execution services. Because we design and build under one roof, projects move forward with fewer conflicts, fewer delays, and better outcomes.

No Gaps. No Excuses. Single-Source Accountability.

When architects hand off drawings to disconnected contractors, mistakes are covered up, material substitutions occur, and the client is stuck in the middle resolving finger-pointing.

At VASTUSHED, our on-site engineers, quantity surveyors, and project managers work side-by-side with our design principals. Every detail is vetted for structural integrity, accurate cost estimation, and reliable handover dates.

Our Execution Strengths
  • Clear milestone-based project planning
  • Multi-tier quality control on-site
  • Reliable, audited timelines and deliverables
  • Direct coordination between design and build teams

Material QA & Structural Testing Matrix

Standard Indian Standards (IS) compliance benchmarks enforced across all VASTUSHED job sites.

REBAR QA PASSED

Fe-550D TMT Reinforcement

  • IS Code: IS 1786:2008
  • Yield Strength: ≥ 550 N/mm²
  • Elongation: ≥ 14.5% Seismic Grade
  • Testing: Ultrasonic Flaw & Weight/Meter
100% Tata Tiscon / Jindal Panther certified.
CONCRETE CRUSH PASSED

M25/M30 Ready-Mix Cube Test

  • IS Code: IS 456 & IS 516
  • Slump Range: 110mm ± 25mm flow
  • 7-Day Test: ≥ 70% characteristic target
  • 28-Day Test: ≥ 30 - 38 N/mm² crushing
Cube cast every 30m³ pour with lab certs.
WATERPROOFING PASSED

Subterranean & Terrace Shield

  • IS Code: IS 3067 & IS 1346
  • Barrier 1: Crystalline penetration slurry
  • Barrier 2: 4mm SBS torch-on membrane
  • Ponding Test: 72-Hour continuous hydro-load
10-Year zero-dampness structural warranty.
MEP PRESSURE PASSED

Megger & Hydrostatic Load

  • IS Code: IS 732 & IS 1239
  • Electrical: 1000V Megger insulation test
  • Plumbing: 10-Bar hydraulic leak hold
  • Earthing: Copper plate < 1.0 Ohm resistance
Concealed lines audited before wall closing.
